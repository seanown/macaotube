MacaoTube

Macau's tourism and culture on YouTube: the official channels of the Tourism Office (澳門旅遊局) and the Cultural Affairs Bureau (文化局), alongside the independent creators people actually watch. Ranked by breakout, engagement and freshness, with festivals, heritage, arts and food lifted.

What's working · median breakout by topic

    How the ranking works. Breakout is a video's views divided by its channel's average views per video, capped at 50×. Engagement is likes plus twice the comments, per view. Freshness halves every 18 months. Independent videos need Macau in the title and at least 1,000 views. Official videos need at least 300 views; sports, public-service and admin videos are left out. Festival, heritage, arts, tradition and food videos get a small boost. Shorts are videos of three minutes or less.
